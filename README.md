# Clocky's Revenge

Explore a world and meet exciting people in Clocky's Revenge. Encounter a Magical Fortune Teller, Stabby Villager Family and Armed Old Lady in a mysterious woods! Talk to these characters and eventually Clocky themselves to learn what happened between you and Clocky to launch them into this explosive state.

## Features: 

- A short game with well crafted dialogue and a small world to explore.

- Limited time for your responses to create stakes

- Different dialogue trees and ways to win and lose

- Custom made art by a small team!
